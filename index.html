Methodology

How this demonstration represents habitat change analysis, and where its limits are.

DATA

Inputs

Publicly available geographic and Earth-observation data can be used as inputs to this kind of analysis. This demonstration uses a prepared sample dataset rather than a live retrieval pipeline.

INDICATORS

Vegetation — NDVI

NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)

Higher NDVI generally indicates denser green vegetation. Lower NDVI can indicate sparse vegetation, bare surfaces, or other non-vegetated conditions.

Water — NDWI

NDWI = (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR)

NDWI is used to approximate surface water extent between two comparison periods. Seasonal variation can materially affect water coverage independent of any longer-term trend.

LAND-COVER CHANGE

Comparing two observation periods

Comparison of environmental layers across different dates can reveal spatial changes. Each analysis compares a baseline and a comparison period selected by the user.

ΔNDVI = NDVI_after − NDVI_before
INTERPRETATION

Change detection thresholds

Areas with significant indicator changes can be flagged for further investigation. This prototype classifies change using fixed demonstration thresholds:

Stable — ΔNDVI > −0.05
Moderate change — −0.20 < ΔNDVI ≤ −0.05
Significant change — ΔNDVI ≤ −0.20
LIMITATIONS

What this demonstration does not account for

  • Seasonal variation can affect vegetation indices.
  • Cloud cover can obscure satellite observations.
  • Resolution varies between datasets.
  • Indicator changes do not automatically establish the cause.
  • Results require contextual interpretation.
  • Field verification may be required.

About

Wildlife Habitat Monitoring

This demonstration explores how publicly available geographic and Earth-observation data can be used to identify environmental changes across a selected area.

  • Spatial selection — choose an example region or draw a custom monitoring area.
  • Environmental indicators — vegetation, water, and built-up demonstration layers.
  • Temporal comparison — compare a baseline and a comparison period.
  • Change visualization — a spatial overlay highlights stable, moderate, and significant change.
  • Areas requiring review — flagged locations for closer inspection.

Results shown in this demonstration are illustrative and should not be interpreted as field-verified observations.

Method

Vegetation change derived from an NDVI-inspired indicator (NIR−Red)/(NIR+Red); water change derived from an NDWI-inspired indicator (Green−NIR)/(Green+NIR); built-up change derived from a land-cover demonstration layer. Values shown are generated by a prepared demonstration dataset, not a live satellite feed.

Limitations

Seasonal variation, cloud cover, sensor and resolution differences, and false positives can all affect index-based change detection. Indicator changes do not automatically establish a cause. Field verification is recommended before acting on any finding in this report.
