Scientific Methodology & Formulas

OBSERVE → COMPARE → CALCULATE → DETECT → PRIORITIZE → VERIFY
NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index)
NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Chlorophyll absorbs visible red light while healthy plant cell structure strongly reflects near-infrared (NIR). A downward transition in NDVI suggests canopy loss, thinning, or leaf senescence.

NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index)
NDWI = (Green - NIR) / (Green + NIR)

Water absorbs near-infrared while reflecting green radiation. NDWI delineates open water bodies and tracks seasonal inundation or drought shrinkage.

Scientific Honesty & Prototype Limitations:

Optical spectral indices are subject to atmospheric haze, sun angle, and agricultural planting cycles. They highlight areas requiring field inspection rather than definitive proof of illegal deforestation.

How is Habitat Health Calculated?

The Habitat Health score (0–100) is a weighted demonstration composite index designed to prioritize landscape-level monitoring:

  • Vegetation Canopy Vitality (35% weight): Evaluates photosynthetic NDVI density against historical baselines.
  • Water Reserve Permanence (25% weight): Tracks surface reservoir and riparian moisture via NDWI.
  • Land-Cover Stability (20% weight): Monitors resistance against bare soil exposure or fringe intrusion.
  • Habitat Patch Connectivity (20% weight): Measures unfragmented movement routes between core patches.
Notice: This is a demonstration composite indicator designed for relative prioritization and does not replace in-situ ecological biodiversity inventories.
